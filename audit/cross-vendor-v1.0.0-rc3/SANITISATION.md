# Sanitisation note

These files are sanitised copies of the independent cross-vendor audit
reports.

Redactions and generalisations are limited to incidental operational
information with no bearing on the mathematical and formal-verification
conclusions, including local hostnames, internal network addresses,
filesystem paths, machine-specific hardware information, temporary build
locations, and internal workflow infrastructure.

No mathematical finding, severity classification, counterexample,
declaration reference, dependency argument, audit-source identity, or final
audit verdict has been altered.

The complete raw audit record is kept by the author and is available upon
request.
